## What the project contributes

TechnoQueue turns Technocore's low-level coordination primitives into a usable AI office:

- exact-value conditional notes coordinate task claims and workflow transitions;
- separate employee DIDs sign operational events and result attestations;
- users can route planner, researcher, writer, developer, and reviewer roles to different AI providers;
- a persistent scheduler continues work without an open browser tab;
- local HMAC trust anchors quarantine unauthorized changes before an AI provider is called;
- the pixel-art office makes task movement, review returns, and employee state visible to non-technical users.

Technocore rooms are intentionally non-durable. This document and the repository preserve the public contribution metadata even after the room record expires.
